The last substantial gap the four-way review found (mockup tag *"Search lands in its slot"* — reviews C and D both ranked it the #1 fidelity gap; the plan audit confirmed the empty-slot copy was redirecting users to the separate top card instead).

### What ships

- **Find inside the open part.** The empty slot carries its own search input + Find button, mirroring the mockup's `.slotsearch`. It runs the **same `searchPlacesText`** call the top search uses with the **same guards**: 2-character floor (inline "Keep typing" hint), `slotSeq` ownership so a slow query can never render into a slot it wasn't typed in (the effect retires in-flight queries whenever the open slot changes), the asymmetric-detour scope sort, and the same `QuotaExhaustedError` copy inline (`role="status"`).
- **A result becomes a candidate for THAT slot — never a direct plan write.** `makeManualCandidate` builds a real `SlotCandidate`: detour minutes via `asymmetricDetourMinutes` against the drawn road, `detourKm` via `asymmetricDetourKm`, budget share via the engine's exported `budgetSharePct` + `dayDetourBudgetMin` with the trip's own travel style and non-rejected stop count — and **honestly-null arrival fields** rather than a fabricated time. Provenance rides the reason line: *"added from this slot's search"*. Fill remains the second explicit act through the existing `fillSlot`.
- **#179 membership grammar enforced on both entry and render**: already-planned / dismissed hits are refused with a toast; duplicates within the slot are refused; `slotCands()` re-validates manual picks at render (added later on the Timeline or dismissed → drops out instead of doubling), engine candidates untouched (manual IDs dedupe against them), manual picks listed first.
- **Corridor-wide discovery stays where it was** — the top "Nearby ideas" card keeps its role; this slot search never touches its state.
- Results cap at 6 with a "refine" line; empty/no-result/quota states all speak.
