### Changed

- **The Map tab keeps its controls in fewer places.** The journey clock opens on request and during add or verified fix previews. Its viewer choice is saved. Scope sits above Drive picks. Source facts, Refresh and help share one footer. Fix holds route advice. The route warning can be dismissed for each trip. Layers and More hold secondary map tools. Permanent helper text is now accessible text or a tooltip. Drive groups route arcs behind a disclosure. Cost labels stay visible on touch screens. The scope uses the shared select.

### Fixed

- **Map facts now agree across the day rail and planning panels.** Filled stops show their scheduled arrival. Only planned detour stops spend the detour budget. Stays, anchors and stops on the route add nothing. Drive counts each place once and compares each measured pick with the budget left after planned detours. When the planned spend is not measured, the count states the scope only. Unknown costs stay separate and Add stays available. Provider order cannot spend the budget. The rail names used detour time, excess time, unknown costs, and plans with no detour stops. Fix reads the travel-day advice from the driving plan. Apply adds empty days through the shared writer. Undo restores the old dates and days after the save succeeds. Card arrival labels stay beside their times. Basemap styles have one source.

- **UI labels use clear separators.** Map labels start with a capital and use short value pairs. App UI text uses a full stop, a colon or a middle dot in place of em dashes. The locked-day notice keeps the exact server text. A test compares it with each migration that writes it. Source checks guard both rules. En dashes in time ranges stay as written. Undo keeps added days that hold later flights or fixed events.

- **Suggestion pins stay on their real place.** At low zoom, the layout moved crowded pins up to 92 px, so some showed on the sea. Now a pick never moves. Picks that overlap join into a count bubble that opens when you zoom in. Lettered Stay picks stay single.

- **The Map tab chrome is calmer and says what is on.** The Layers menu has labelled sections and a Flat, Relief and 3D switch. Each layer and idea type has a real on or off switch. A dot on the Layers button shows a layer that differs from its default. On a wide screen, only the day rail opens by default. Each day on the map is one pill in its own colour. The day strip wraps and does not scroll. Group badges are white chips with a teal ring. The map credit sits in the map corner. Panels slide in, and the suggestions rise again on each lens change. The hidden help button is gone. The live states show on top of the suggestions: over limit, rain, no picks and search failed.
