### Fixed

- **A stop mark now stays on its place when you zoom or pan the map.** A crowded trip, such as 34 stops over Goa, no longer spreads its stop marks off their coordinates. None land in the sea. At low zoom the stops overlap, and the active day's stops draw on top. The time pills, name tags, road labels and pick words slide a short way or hide when they cannot fit. They hide while the camera zooms and come back when it stops, so nothing jumps. A pan changes no layout. On a phone the labels no longer pile up. The map control column now ends above the journey ribbon and scrolls when the map is short. Its filter menu opens inside the map, above the other cards. The dense sample trip (`scenario=dense`) in the dev fixture shows the case. (#889)
