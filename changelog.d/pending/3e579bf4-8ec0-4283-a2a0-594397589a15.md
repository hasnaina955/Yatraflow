### Added

- **Toasts now show a kind icon.** An `ok` toast shows a teal check circle. An `err` toast shows an alert icon. A neutral toast shows no icon. The icon is decorative and hidden from screen readers. (#1053)

### Changed

- **Toasts settle 3 px when they enter.** The pill rises 8 px and fades on `--motion-med` and `--ease-paper`, overshoots by 3 px, then rests. The check icon pops on `--ease-settle` inside the small-pop rule. (#1053)
- **Toasts now leave with a real exit.** On dismiss, Undo or timer end, the toast leaves. It fades and sinks 6 px on `--motion-fast` and `--ease-exit`, then unmounts after that token. A held toast does not leave. The dwell timers, the hover and focus hold and the live region are unchanged. (#1053)
- **The Undo button has a 44 px tap height.** The label reads "Undo". (#1053)
- **The ok and undo toasts use paper at night.** In dark mode the fill is `#E8EFF5` and the text is `#142329`. The check and the Undo label are teal. The `err` toast keeps the danger fill so an error stays unmistakable. Reduced motion shows and removes a toast at once. (#1053)
