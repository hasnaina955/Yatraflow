### Added

- **The phone web app now has the bottom tab bar, with Add in the centre.** At phone width, a signed-in user sees Home, My trips, Add, Explore and Profile at the bottom. Profile shows the same unread badge as the native app. The bar moves away while the keyboard, a sheet or a dialog is open. On the full-screen Map the bar moves away, and the round Add button stays. Pages get bottom space, so the bar does not cover the last row. Desktop and signed-out pages do not change. (#1064)
