### Added

- **Phone stops open one bottom sheet instead of a small popover.** At widths of 720px or less, a stop's ⋯ control opens a sheet with a drag handle, a close button, the stop name and its real day, clock and position. The desktop popover does not change.
- **The sheet carries the stop status and actions.** A three-way status group uses Confirmed, Maybe and Needs booking from the shared status list, with the current status marked. Edit details, Move to another day with real day numbers, Reorder with disabled ends, and Show on the map use the existing paths. Remove goes through the impact preview and leaves an Undo after Keep.
- **The sheet follows the phone access and motion rules.** Controls have a 44px target floor, focus moves in and returns to the ⋯ control on close, Escape and a downward drag close the sheet, the lower edge uses safe-area padding, and the dark theme uses the shared tokens. Entrance and exit use the named motion tokens with an immediate reduced-motion fallback.
