### Fixed

- **The Goa, Rajasthan and Meghalaya calculators use only rates with a source.** Meghalaya drops the unsupported registration fee and raises the SUV rate to the market range. Its guide price is for the whole group. Rajasthan uses the Jaipur petrol price and the documented route distances. Goa raises the low ends that were below the market. The default range per person is now Goa ₹7,700 – ₹12,700, Rajasthan ₹15,100 – ₹25,100 and Meghalaya ₹12,300 – ₹19,300. The Ladakh numbers do not change.
