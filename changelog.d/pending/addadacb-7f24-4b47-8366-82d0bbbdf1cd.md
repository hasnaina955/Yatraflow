### Fixed

- **The Stay lens no longer calls a busy evening free.** The free evening now starts after the last stop that the day schedule places in it. A day with a beach swim and a late dinner after check-in shows no free evening. The deck says that no free time is left, and the header says "no free evening".

- **The Plan lens no longer puts a meal in the wrong slot.** A stop whose title names breakfast, lunch or dinner fills that meal's slot and never another one. Other meal stops use the shared day schedule. Opening hours apply only when the schedule is absent.

- **The Fix pill now counts the problems that the Fix deck lists.** A problem counts even when no safe fix exists. The pill and the deck header now read one rule. An unread day shows no count.

- **The day rail no longer shows "Leave" after a stop.** The departure row is the first row of the day. Only a breakfast at last night's stay can come before it. A stop saved at the start place still shows zero distance. Its position needs a separate correction in the trip data.

- **The detour budget uses planned stops only.** The rail names the excess when planned detours exceed the limit. Unknown planned costs and a plan with no detour stops each show their own message.
