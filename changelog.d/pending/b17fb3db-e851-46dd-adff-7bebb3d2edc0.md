### Added

- **The Map tab is now one lens workspace on one engine schedule, and its Fix lens offers only verified changes.** Four lenses sit behind one switch with honest counts. An empty lens counts 0. An unscanned or failed lens shows no count. Each lens opens one deck header and one place card. Pick cards show the place's own facts only. The left rail follows the active lens. A timed day list sits beside the map. On a phone, Plan opens the day's list. Search and the lens switcher share one row. The map fills the screen under a sheet with peek, half and full heights. Drive draws the plan on the road. Clock ticks run every 20 km. The lunch window shows its name, hours and kilometre. Stay draws both reach rings, letters three picks A to C, and opens pick A in full. Gap rings each open part of the day. Fix lists verified fixes only, and each fix says what it clears. Apply writes a fix. Preview on map shows it first. Keep saves through the shared writer, with one Undo. Add preview shows the stop order, the moved clocks, the engine's risk and its fix before any add. A stop clears only when its own warning is gone. Map marks are round stop discs in the day colour, and the key explains each mark. Stays have house marks. Fixed bookings have lock badges. A problem has a coral badge. Every clock in map text follows the 12 h or 24 h setting. A fixed booking reads its own clock, such as the 15:30 boat. The approved day palette ships on the Linen and Night basemaps. Map controls share one 44px target floor. The journey ribbon scrubs across midnight. Map and Timeline read one per-day journey projection. The corridor scan asks for nature, faith and culture places. Net added minutes compute per suggestion row, but no view shows them yet. Live position stays private and shows only on the trip day. The map keys its reach rings by content, which ends a render loop. (#889, #911)

- **A focused test run now selects with git.** Two hand-written focused lists had omitted tests the change touched. A green local run then met a red full gate. `npm run test:changed` unions the test files changed vs the batch base. It adds staged, unstaged, untracked and explicit `--extra` paths. It passes vitest's exit code through. A source change that selects no test now exits 1. It never passes an empty green. A dangling `--base` is a usage error, not a silent default. A deleted test path is never passed to vitest. The full gate still runs everything.

- **A requested place family now reaches the providers.** The facade forwards it to the Google along-route scan and to the single free OpenStreetMap scan. Default scans keep their exact queries and request count. Nothing enables family selection on its own.

- **Free-mode OpenStreetMap hits now carry their confirmed scenic family facts.** A waterfall, lake or viewpoint keeps its existing category, description, identity and coordinates. Only tags the family helper already understands attach a family and subtype. Fuel, food, lodging and worship hits stay unchanged, and no new request is made.

- **Scenic places now classify by family with confirmed subtype facts.** Provider type, then evidenced name, then OSM tags decide the family. A waterfall fits a stretch break, and a viewpoint cannot fill a fuel gap. Heritage, water and views keep their generic facts until the source rows are complete.

- **Road-measured detours now have one validated fact surface.** A baseline route and a via route through a place give the directed delta. Negative, non-finite or missing results stay unknown. A stale generation cannot replace a newer measurement. The helper ships with an injected transport, so no live routing call starts until rights and limits are agreed.

### Fixed

- **The rail's "km off" now shows the same detour the corridor and the omnibar use.** The alternative pool used a straight line to the anchors, so one place could show two detours. The Map now passes its one upstream detour into the rail. An unknown km stays unknown, and a known zero stays zero. A new source test and a behaviour test pin the single source.

- **Drive suggestions now show a realistic arrival and the day's own budget share.** An estimate adds only the outward leg. A measured leg needs its matching context; unknown evidence stays unknown. The rail chip, card and marker share one own-day value, and no unknown day borrows Day 0.

- **A place's confirmed visit length now drives the fit, card, add, vote draft and slack prompt.** A waterfall, temple or park uses its confirmed minutes; explicit overrides and incomplete, heritage or non-family fallbacks stay unchanged.

- **The Map workspace no longer crashes when a style is mid-load.** The detour spur moved out of `TripMap` into a child of the map provider. It now waits on the provider readiness (map loaded and style loaded) instead of the map's camera latch. The 4 s camera watchdog still opens the fit on a stalled style. The spur re-adds itself after a theme swap and writes the latest suggestion. Lifecycle cleanup removes only its own layer and source. A focused test drives the production helper with a map double that throws on a premature write. The test shows that removing the readiness guard reproduces the loading error. The app browser check stays unrun. Full Map V2 acceptance stays open.

- **A discarded render no longer clears unchanged day and card reuse.** The journey model keeps its committed reuse base until React commits. Returning to the committed trip reuses the exact day and card objects.

- **Map suggestions now charge the return drive once, so the card, the ranker and the day budget agree.** An 8 km spur at 40 km/h shows 24 minutes, not 12 or 48. `asymmetricDetourMinutes` now returns the drive out and back. The ranker weighs that fact once, the day budget spends it once, and the arrival clock keeps the one-way leg. Supplied road evidence still wins. Missing, negative or non-finite distance stays unknown, and it never reads as free.

- **Opening verdicts now use the visit's local weekday and every known interval.** Google hours were read from the first period only. A split day collapsed to its morning block, and a Monday closure read as open. `providers/hours.ts` parses all weekday-attributable periods and keeps split and overnight spans. It separates a known closure from missing or unknown hours. The scorer uses the verdict when the projection supplies a visit date. A fetch date stays a freshness stamp, not proof that the place is open on a future date.

- **Map suggestions now score opening hours against the trip's own day.** The visit date comes from the trip calendar. The intended visit length comes from the pacing the add path already uses. A Monday closure and a visit that overruns closing time are scored, not guessed. Cards show the intervals that apply to that day, so a split place no longer shows only its first period. The drive rail now renders the presentation adapter's rows.

- **Map suggestions now use the arrival clock they show, and rain cannot make a wrong kind eligible.** The hours check adds the outward leg before it tests a close. It uses the directed measured leg when its clock matches. A closed weekday still shows its note when no interval is provable. Rain adjusts the score only: it never turns zero fit into a valid need. A measured road detour is charged once, and its arrival leg is used instead of half the total. Measured evidence is bound to the hit, origin, route and departure clock. A mismatch shows unknown arrival, and a stale stamp is removed.
