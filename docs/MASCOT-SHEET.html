Chakra, round 4

Round 4 adds twelve expressions and eleven looping animations. Chakra keeps the koel's pale bill and long tail, and marigold belongs only to the route dot.

Meet Chakra

An Asian koel, the bird whose call means the monsoon is near. Chakra flies ahead, checks the route and tells you when to stop for chai.

  • EyeKoel red, with two highlights
  • TuftTwo curls; the lagoon one shows mood
  • BillPale, like a real koel's
  • TailLong, like a koel's; it becomes the route
  • BlushSoft pink on the cheek
  • FeelCalm, curious, a little cheeky
