Paper settles. It never bounces around.

One duration scale and five easings replace today's two scales (--t-* and --motion-*) and 67 one-off keyframes. Everything on this board is live: hover, press and click the demos.

Cause, then effectMotion starts where the finger or cursor is. A sheet rises from the button that opened it; a stop flies to the day it moved to.
Fast to feel, slower to travelFeedback (press, hover, toggle) lands in under 160ms. Only things that move across the screen take longer, up to 560ms.
Weight like paperLifts are 2px, presses shrink to 97%, entrances rise 8px. A 3% settle overshoot only on small delights: hearts, toasts, stamps.
Calm by defaultNothing loops unless it is loading or live. With reduced motion, movement becomes a 120ms fade and route lines appear already drawn.

Reduced motionWhen the phone or OS asks for less motion, the app keeps the meaning and drops the movement:
• Slides, lifts and pops become a 120ms fade. • Routes appear drawn, pins appear placed, numbers swap. • Skeleton sheen stops; a static warm block stays. • Map fly-to becomes a jump cut with a 120ms fade.
Today's tokens → new
--motion-fast 120ms→--dur-quick --t-fast 180 · --motion-med 180→--dur-quick / --dur-base --motion-slow 240→--dur-base --t-med 300 · --t-slow 440→--dur-calm --motion-slower 560→--dur-scenic --ease-out · --ease-soft→--ease-paper --stagger-step 60ms→40ms, 6 max
Rules that keep it calm
• Animate only transform and opacity, except height in accordions. • One thing moves at a time per region; no parallel entrances. • Never animate on every keystroke or filter tick; debounce 150ms. • Loops only for live states: loading, "viewing now", recording.
