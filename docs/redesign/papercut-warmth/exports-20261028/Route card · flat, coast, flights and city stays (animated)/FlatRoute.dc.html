One route card, four ways to draw the road

The Leh card draws the road by height because height is the story there. On a flat trip that chart would be a straight line. So the card keeps the same frame and changes what the strip shows: the land you drive through, the legs of the journey, or the neighbourhoods of one base.

The road, by height Road trip with 1,000 m or more of climb, or any pass above 2,500 m. Done · P12 Overview
